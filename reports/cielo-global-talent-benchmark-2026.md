# Cielo — 2026 Global Talent Benchmark: TA Tech Investment vs. Hiring Agility

**Source:** https://www.cielotalent.com/resources/press-room/ta-tech-investment-rose-scale-remains-a-challenge/
**Published:** 2026-10-14
**Data:** 396 enterprise HR leaders at organizations with 2,500+ employees in the U.S., U.K., and India; survey fielded July 14–September 1, 2026 by Researchscape International; results weighted by nominal GDP
**Period:** Current-state assessment of TA technology and workforce strategy as of mid-2026
**Best for:** Enterprise TA tech ROI benchmarking, skills framework maturity, workforce agility assessment, TA business value measurement

---

## TA Technology Investment

- **87%** of enterprise employers **increased TA tech investment** over the past 12 months
- Despite high investment, the majority of organizations **still struggle to translate tech spending into greater hiring agility**
- Tech investment has not resolved the core challenge: proving business value from talent acquisition

## Skills Frameworks and Workforce Structure

- Only **29%** of organizations have a **dynamic skills framework** deployed across the enterprise
- **45%** are still in the process of **moving away from traditional job structures** — indicating most large employers remain in transition
- Skills-based hiring has broad buy-in but limited operational implementation

## Measuring TA Business Value

- Only **23% of organizations track TA outcomes** tied directly to enterprise value, business growth, or the bottom line
- Most TA functions still measure activity (time-to-fill, cost-per-hire) rather than business impact
- The measurement gap is the primary reason TA struggles to secure strategic resources

## Key Conclusions

- Enterprise TA tech spending is near-universal (87% increased investment) but is failing to deliver proportional hiring agility
- The skills framework gap is critical: 71% of large employers lack a dynamic skills framework, limiting their ability to match people to work
- TA's inability to tie outcomes to business value (only 23% do) leaves it vulnerable to budget cuts despite high investment
- Most large enterprises are still mid-transition from job-based to skills-based operating models
- The data suggests TA technology ROI is being left on the table due to measurement and adoption gaps, not technology limitations
