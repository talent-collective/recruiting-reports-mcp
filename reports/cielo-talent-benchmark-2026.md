# Cielo — 2026 Talent Benchmark: TA Tech Investment vs. Hiring Agility

**Source:** https://www.cielotalent.com/resources/press-room/ta-tech-investment-rose-scale-remains-a-challenge/
**Published:** 2026-10-14
**Data:** 396 enterprise HR leaders at organizations with 2,500+ employees; United States, United Kingdom, and India; survey conducted by Researchscape International
**Period:** Survey fielded July 14–September 1, 2026; results weighted by country nominal GDP
**Best for:** Enterprise TA tech ROI benchmarking, skills framework adoption rates, hiring agility measurement, agentic AI readiness in large organizations

---

## Technology Investment vs. Hiring Agility

- **87%** of enterprise HR leaders increased talent acquisition tech investment over the past 12 months
- Yet only **31%** can scale sourcing and screening without adding recruiter headcount
- **56%** say AI gives them at least some hiring agility — but capacity gains have not kept pace with spending
- Only **23%** track talent acquisition outcomes tied directly to enterprise value, business growth, or the bottom line
- Cielo CEO Marissa Geist: "Adding AI to the same hiring model is not transformation"

## Skills Frameworks and Job Architecture

- **29%** have implemented a dynamic skills framework enterprise-wide
- **45%** are still transitioning away from traditional job structures toward skills-based hiring models
- The majority of enterprises lack the infrastructure to match workforce capability to business demand at speed

## Recruiter Capacity and Scaling

- AI investment has not yet translated into eliminating recruiter headcount constraints for most organizations
- Only about 1 in 3 enterprises can flex sourcing and screening volume without proportional headcount growth
- The report frames this as the core gap between TA technology spend and hiring agility

## Key Conclusions

- Enterprise TA tech spending is near-universal (87%) but ROI in hiring agility remains limited — only 31% can scale without adding recruiters
- Measurement is a critical blind spot: 77% of enterprises cannot tie TA performance to enterprise value
- Skills-based hiring infrastructure is early-stage: less than a third have deployed enterprise-wide skills frameworks
- The bottleneck has shifted from technology access to talent operating model design and change management
- Agentic AI era requires redesigning the hiring model itself, not just layering AI tools on existing processes
