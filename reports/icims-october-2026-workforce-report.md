# iCIMS — October 2026 Workforce Report: Holiday Hiring Cooldown Pressures Retailers

**Source:** https://www.icims.com/company/newsroom/octoberinsights2026/
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Data:** 3.1M+ global iCIMS platform users; 691M+ candidate profiles
**Period:** September 2026 labor market data
**Best for:** Retail seasonal hiring strategy, frontline candidate re-engagement, holiday workforce planning

---

## Labor Market Context

- Frontline applications ended September **18% below baseline** — candidate supply continues to lag demand
- Overall job openings and hiring remain in a multi-month slow period heading into Q4
- Holiday sales growth estimates vary widely — from 3.5% to 6%+ YoY — creating uncertainty in seasonal staffing plans

## Retail Hiring Trends

- Retail **time to fill held at 37 days** in September, remaining in the mid-30-day range throughout 2026
- Gen Z candidates (ages 18–24) made up **40% of retail applicants** in September 2026, up from 37% a year earlier
- Every age group 45 and older **lost share** of the retail applicant pool YoY
- Retailer strategy highlight: Urban Outfitters re-engages former employees and past applicants via iCIMS to fill seasonal roles — reducing time-to-fill and cost-per-hire

## AI and Candidate Re-Engagement

- With application volumes below baseline, retailers are being pressured to use AI and candidate re-engagement to surface qualified candidates faster
- The gap between open roles and available applicants is forcing TA teams to work existing candidate databases rather than rely on inbound
- AI-powered screening and automated re-engagement sequences are emerging as primary tactics for holiday ramp-up

## Key Conclusions

- Frontline hiring is entering the holiday season with a structural supply deficit — applications are 18% below baseline
- Retail time-to-fill stability (37 days) masks increasing difficulty filling roles, because fewer qualified candidates are applying
- Gen Z is becoming the dominant demographic in retail candidate pools, shifting sourcing and communication strategies
- Candidate re-engagement (past applicants and former employees) is the highest-leverage tactic retailers have available this cycle
- Employers who invested in AI-powered ATS workflows earlier in 2026 are better positioned to close seasonal roles faster
