# iCIMS — October 2026 Workforce Report: Holiday Hiring Cooldown Pressures Retailers to Move Qualified Candidates Faster

**Source:** https://www.icims.com/company/newsroom/octoberinsights2026/
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Data:** Proprietary iCIMS platform data from 3M+ global platform users and 691M+ candidate profiles
**Period:** September 2026 data (compared to September 2025 baseline)
**Best for:** Holiday season hiring strategy, retail and transportation frontline talent, Gen Z candidate engagement, AI re-engagement campaigns, Q4 2026 workforce planning

## U.S. Labor Market Snapshot — September 2026

| Metric | Value | vs. September 2025 Baseline |
|--------|-------|------------------------------|
| U.S. job openings | — | +3% |
| U.S. hires | — | −7% |
| Frontline applications | — | −18% |
| Applicants per opening | 29 | Lowest of 2026 |
| Retail time to fill | 37 days | — |

- U.S. hiring fell 7% below the September 2025 baseline — labor market cooling is now broadening across sectors
- Job openings remain marginally positive (+3%) but hiring is contracting, widening the demand-supply inversion
- Applicants per opening hit 29 — the lowest point of 2026 — leaving no buffer for slow screening or extended hiring timelines

## Retail and Frontline Hiring: Holiday Season Under Pressure

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Frontline application volume vs. baseline | −18% |
| Sales associate and cashier application volume | −18% below baseline |
| Retail time to fill | 37 days |
| Share of holiday hiring pool: Gen Z | 40% |

- Frontline applications ended September 18% below baseline — a sharp drop entering the highest-demand season for retail and transportation
- High-volume roles (sales associates, cashiers) are experiencing the steepest application volume declines
- Retailers face a compressed runway: with applications down and competition for available candidates rising, time-to-screen becomes the decisive variable
- Gen Z now constitutes 40% of the holiday hiring pool — EVP alignment, digital-native hiring experiences, and AI tools matter more than ever for this cohort

## Cooling Labor Market Signals

- September's data represents a broader deceleration — not just retail — with overall U.S. hiring 7% below baseline while openings only grew 3%
- The inversion between openings and hires (demand up, hiring down) is sustained and may reflect structural caution, budget constraints, or longer decision cycles
- Retail time to fill of 37 days means that employers starting hiring campaigns in October have little margin before holiday season peak demand arrives

## Strategic Imperatives for Q4 2026

- **AI-powered screening:** With applicants per opening at 29 (lowest of the year), speed-to-screen separates retailers who fill roles from those who miss the seasonal window
- **Candidate re-engagement:** With new application flow down 18%, reactivating pipeline from prior-cycle applicants is a necessary supplement to top-of-funnel efforts
- **Gen Z hiring experience:** 40% of the holiday pool is Gen Z — mobile-first applications, fast feedback loops, and transparent process communication are non-negotiable
- **Transportation co-planning:** Retail hiring outcomes are linked to transportation staffing; both sectors face similar application compression

## Key Conclusions

- U.S. hires fell 7% below September 2025 baseline while openings rose just 3% — the labor market cooldown is sustained and broad, not seasonal
- Frontline application volume dropped 18% below baseline heading into the peak holiday hiring season — a significant headwind for retailers and transportation employers
- Applicants per opening reached 29, the lowest of 2026, eliminating any slack in screening and offer timelines
- The holiday hiring pool is 40% Gen Z, demanding digital-native hiring experiences and fast decision cycles
- TA teams must combine AI-powered screening, active candidate re-engagement from existing pipelines, and accelerated offer decisioning to meet Q4 headcount targets
