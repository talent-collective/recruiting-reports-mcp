# iCIMS — October 2026 Workforce Report: Holiday Hiring Cooldown Pressures Retailers to Move Candidates Faster

**Source:** https://www.icims.com/company/newsroom/octoberinsights2026/
**Published:** 2026-10-08
**Data:** Proprietary iCIMS platform data from 3M+ global platform users and 691M+ candidate profiles
**Period:** September 2026 data (released October 2026)
**Best for:** Holiday hiring strategy, retail and frontline talent pipeline, candidate pool size trends, age demographic shifts in retail applicants, AI-powered candidate re-engagement

---

## Labor Market Snapshot — September 2026

| Metric | Value | Change |
|--------|-------|--------|
| U.S. hiring | — | −7% vs. September 2025 baseline |
| U.S. job openings | — | +3% above baseline |
| Application volume (high-volume roles) | — | −18% below baseline |
| Applicants per opening | 29 | Down from 40 a year earlier |
| Retail time to fill | 37 days | Flat (held mid-30s all year) |

- U.S. hiring ended September 7% below the September 2025 baseline while openings stayed 3% above it
- Applicants per opening fell to 29, sharply down from 40 a year earlier — the candidate pool has thinned significantly
- Application volume for high-volume roles (sales associates, cashiers) ended September 18% below baseline

## Retail and Frontline Hiring Dynamics

- Retail time to fill held at **37 days** — consistent throughout 2026, but capacity to absorb holiday volume is at risk
- The candidate pool entering the holiday season is both smaller and younger than a year ago
- Retailers face a compressed window: smaller pool + same time-to-fill target + higher volume need = elevated conversion pressure

## Applicant Demographic Shifts

| Age Group | September 2026 | September 2025 |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| 18–24 | 40% of retail applicants | 37% |
| 45+ | Declining share | Larger share |

- Applicants aged 18–24 made up **40% of retail applicants** in September 2026, up from 37% a year earlier
- Every age group 45 and older lost share in the retail applicant pool
- The younger skew affects onboarding timelines and seasonality of availability (college/school schedules)

## Strategic Recommendations from iCIMS

- **Re-engage past applicants**: Retailers should identify and re-engage former employees and prior applicants who were strong fits — a known pool reduces screening time
- **Speed up early-stage communication**: Faster initial outreach and screening decisions before competing offers land
- **Use AI for screening volume**: Automated screening tools are critical when pools are smaller but candidate quality assessment demands remain high

## Key Conclusions

- The 2026 holiday hiring season begins with the most constrained frontline candidate pool in recent years — applicants per opening fell from 40 to 29 year-over-year
- Application volume for high-volume retail roles is 18% below baseline, meaning the typical strategy of relying on seasonal surge applications will not work as well as in prior years
- U.S. hiring is 7% below the year-ago baseline while openings remain slightly elevated — the demand-supply imbalance continues into fall
- The retail applicant pool is skewing younger (40% aged 18–24, up from 37%), which has implications for training time and schedule flexibility
- Candidate re-engagement and AI-powered communication speed are the two highest-leverage tactics for retailers navigating holiday hiring with a thinner, younger applicant base
