# iCIMS — October 2026 Workforce Report: Holiday Hiring Cooldown Pressures Retailers to Move Qualified Candidates Faster

## September 2026 U.S. Hiring Overview

- U.S. hiring fell **7% below the September 2025 baseline** in September 2026
- U.S. job openings remained **3% above baseline** — openings continue to outpace actual hires for a third consecutive month
- Hiring, openings, and applications all moved downward together in September for the first time this year

## Frontline and Retail Hiring

- Frontline application volume ended September **18% below baseline** — the softest point in 2026
- Applicants per open frontline role fell to **29** — the lowest of the year
- High-volume hiring stayed above the September 2025 baseline from March through August, then fell **8% below baseline** in September
- Retail time to fill held steady at **37 days**
- Candidates aged 18–24 made up **40%** of retail applicants in September, up from 37% a year earlier
- Every candidate cohort aged 45 and older lost share of retail applicant pool year over year

## Candidate Pool and Re-engagement Context

- Declining frontline application volume puts pressure on employers to work existing pipelines
- iCIMS highlights three key re-engagement levers for Q4:
  1. Re-engage last year's seasonal workforce
  2. Revisit qualified candidates already in the ATS pipeline
  3. Activate employee referrals
- Urban Outfitters case: keeps seasonal hiring moving by re-engaging former employees and past applicants via iCIMS platform

## EMEA Context

- EMEA hiring remained relatively stronger than the U.S. in Q3 — described as "the heat continues in EMEA" as U.S. cools entering Q4
- Full EMEA data breakdowns available in the downloadable report

## Key Conclusions

- **Holiday hiring has shifted left:** Frontline and retail employers can no longer rely on last-minute application surges to fill seasonal roles as they could in 2022–2024.
- **Openings-hires gap persists:** Job openings consistently outpacing hires for three consecutive months signals employer caution and slower conversion, not just weak demand.
- **Age skew in retail applicant pool:** Rising 18–24 share of retail applicants may reflect Gen Z's continued willingness to take frontline work, but the absolute pool is shrinking.
- **Re-engagement outperforms cold sourcing in Q4:** With application volume at yearly lows, existing talent pools (past seasonal workers, prior applicants, referrals) become the highest-ROI source.
- **Time-to-fill stability (37 days) is deceptive:** Flat time-to-fill against a backdrop of falling applications suggests employers are accepting lower screening thresholds, not moving faster.
