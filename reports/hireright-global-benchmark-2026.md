# HireRight — 2026 Global Benchmark Report

**Source:** https://www.hireright.com/benchmark2026
**Published:** 2026-06-30
**Data:** 1,900+ HR, risk, and talent acquisition professionals worldwide
**Period:** Survey conducted February 18 – March 6, 2026
**Best for:** Background screening benchmarks, AI-enabled hiring risk, candidate discrepancy rates, post-hire screening trends, remote-vs-office hiring decisions

---

## Background Screening Benchmarks

- **Candidate discrepancies:** Most companies found at least one candidate discrepancy during background screening in the last 12 months
- **High-volume discrepancy rate:** 13% of employers globally found on average at least one discrepancy per every five candidates screened
- **Top discrepancy type:** Employment verifications — the area most likely to uncover inconsistencies globally
- **Most common inconsistency types:** Undisclosed criminal convictions; education and employment record discrepancies
- **Enterprise-sized organizations** reported the highest rates of candidate discrepancies

## Screening Provider Selection Criteria

| Priority | Finding |
|----------|---------|
| #1 globally | Accuracy and quality of results |
| Top risk (EMEA/APAC) | Detecting behavior or history misaligned with company values (outranks "cost of a bad hire") |
| Top risk (North America) | Cost of a bad hire remains primary risk driver |

## AI in Hiring: Adoption and Attitudes

- AI adoption in HR functions continues to grow across all regions
- **APAC** organizations were the most likely to report using AI tools to support HR functions
- Candidate use of generative AI during the application process: **APAC** respondents (~50%) largely view it **positively**; North American respondents were largely **neutral or unsure**
- Growing employer concern about **AI-enabled hiring risks**, including deepfakes and credential fraud
- Increased adoption of **identity verification checks** and **post-hire screening programs** globally in response to AI fraud concerns

## Remote Work and Workforce Location Trends

- Organizations globally reported **declining plans to hire fully remote employees** in 2026
- Increasing employer expectations for **office-based or hybrid workforces**
- Despite this shift, recruitment challenges tied to remote work expectations persist
- Identity fraud concerns are increasing as virtual hiring remains common for many roles

## Key Conclusions

- **Candidate misrepresentation is widespread:** Most employers found discrepancies; employment verification remains the weakest link, suggesting rigorous screening is still an operational necessity, not a checkbox
- **AI fraud risk is emerging as a screening priority:** AI-generated credentials, deepfakes in remote interviews, and falsified employment histories are driving identity verification investment
- **Regional divergence on AI in hiring:** APAC is further ahead in AI adoption and candidate AI acceptance; North America lags in both sentiment and deployment
- **Return-to-office is reshaping risk profiles:** As RTO mandates increase, employer risk calculus shifts back toward on-site-relevant checks (background, values alignment) and away from remote-centric protocols
- **Enterprise organizations bear the highest screening burden:** Larger organizations experience the most candidate discrepancies, reinforcing the need for systematic screening at scale
