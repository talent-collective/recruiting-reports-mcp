# HireRight — 2026 Global Benchmark Report

**Source:** https://www.hireright.com/resources/global-benchmark-report
**Published:** 2026-06-30
**Data:** 1,900+ HR, risk, and talent acquisition professionals surveyed worldwide
**Period:** Survey conducted February 18 – March 6, 2026
**Best for:** Background screening adoption benchmarks, candidate discrepancy rates, identity fraud in hiring, AI-in-hiring attitudes by region, workforce risk prioritization

---

## Background Screening Practices

- ~90% of organizations globally conduct criminal record checks on new hires — the most common type of check
- Only 3 in 5 (60%) respondents say their company conducts identity checks as part of pre-employment screening
- Employment verifications are the area most likely to uncover inconsistencies globally
- Enterprise-sized organizations report the highest rates of candidate discrepancies

## Candidate Discrepancies

- Most companies uncovered candidate discrepancies during background screening in the last 12 months
- 13% of respondents found on average at least one discrepancy per every five candidates screened
- Undisclosed criminal convictions and education/employment discrepancies are the most common types of inconsistency identified

## Identity Fraud and AI-Enabled Hiring Risks

- 1 in 6 respondents say their organization has experienced ID fraud as part of the hiring process
- Organizations are increasing adoption of identity verification checks and post-hire screening programs in response to growing fraud concerns
- AI-enabled hiring risks and workforce trust concerns are driving expanded screening programs globally

## AI and Technology in Hiring

- AI adoption in HR continues to grow, but attitudes toward candidates using AI tools during applications vary by region:
  - **North America:** Respondents largely neutral or unsure about candidates using generative AI during applications
  - **APAC:** Nearly half (≈50%) view candidate use of AI tools positively
- Organizations globally reported declining plans to hire fully remote employees in 2026, with increasing expectations for office-based or hybrid workforces

## Workforce Risk Priorities by Region

- **Global:** Accuracy and quality of results remain the top priority for choosing an employment screening provider
- **EMEA and APAC:** Detecting behavior or histories misaligned with company values outranks "cost of a bad hire" as a top risk organizations aim to mitigate through screening
- **North America:** Cost of a bad hire remains a leading concern driving screening investment

## Key Conclusions

- Background screening discrepancies are widespread: most employers found at least one inconsistency in the past 12 months, with enterprise orgs seeing the highest rates
- Identity fraud is a real and growing risk: 1 in 6 employers has experienced it directly in the hiring process, yet 40% still don't conduct identity checks
- Regional divergence in AI attitudes: APAC employers are far more accepting of candidates using AI tools than their North American counterparts, creating compliance complexity for global TA teams
- Value misalignment is a top hiring risk in EMEA/APAC, suggesting that cultural fit and values screening are growing concerns beyond traditional credentialing
- The shift away from fully remote hiring is accelerating globally in 2026, with implications for candidate pools and screening scope
