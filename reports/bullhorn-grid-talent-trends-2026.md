# Bullhorn — GRID 2026 Talent Trends Report

**Source:** https://www.bullhorn.com/grid/2026-industry-trends/report/
**Published:** September 23, 2026
**Data:** ~2,300 professionals who worked with a staffing/recruitment firm in the past 3 years; 16th annual edition; global coverage across North America, UK/Ireland, DACH, Benelux, APAC; supplemented by qualitative interviews with industry leaders and candidates
**Period:** Survey conducted 2026; reflects 2025 performance data
**Best for:** AI ROI benchmarks in staffing, AI voice interview candidate acceptance, staffing revenue performance by AI-adoption tier, candidate agency-usage trends

---

## AI Adoption and Revenue Performance

| AI Adoption Level | Revenue Impact |
|-------------------|---------------|
| Firms using AI at any stage | 3.5–4.5x more likely to have grown revenue |
| Firms using AI embedded in ATS (revenue grew 25%+) | 78% use embedded AI tools |
| Firms using AI embedded in ATS (revenue declined 10%+) | 51% use embedded AI tools |
| Firms using AI screening reporting KPI improvement >25% | 55% |
| Leaders equipped to guide AI adoption | ~40% more likely to have achieved revenue growth |

## AI Voice Interview Candidate Acceptance

| Metric | 2026 | 2025 (prior year) |
|--------|------|-------------------|
| Candidates rating AI voice interviews as good as or better than human interviews | 92% | 88% |
| Candidates with positive AI experience who would work with that firm again | 93% | 93% |
| Candidates who encountered AI through their staffing firm | 69% | 50% |
| Candidates who rated their AI experience positively | 81% | 77% |

## Staffing Industry Financial Performance

- 56% of firms reported revenue growth in 2025 (up from 40% the prior year)
- Top-performing firms are 4x more likely to leverage AI than lower performers
- 43% of firms expect only modest economic improvement in 2026
- Tight talent pools and falling job volumes identified as the top dual challenge for 2026

## Candidate Job Search Behavior

- Only 35% of candidates turn to recruitment agencies first when job searching (continued decline from prior years)
- Agency use is declining particularly among Gen Z (consistent with 2025 report)
- Candidates who disengage cite: lengthy processes and poor communication as primary drivers

## Key Conclusions

- AI has crossed from "emerging innovation" to baseline expectation in the staffing industry: the revenue gap between AI-adopting and non-adopting firms is widening fast
- Candidate acceptance of AI voice interviews reached 92% in 2026 — up 4 percentage points year-over-year — making AI voice a viable first-touch screening method at scale
- The correlation between AI embedding in ATS and 25%+ revenue growth (78% vs. 51% for declining firms) is the clearest ROI signal yet for staffing technology investment
- Candidate agency usage continues to fall — only 35% turn to agencies first — placing higher competitive pressure on staffing firms to differentiate through speed, specialization, and AI-assisted candidate experience
- 2026 market headwinds (tight talent pools, falling job volumes) will intensify the performance gap between AI-enabled and traditional staffing firms
