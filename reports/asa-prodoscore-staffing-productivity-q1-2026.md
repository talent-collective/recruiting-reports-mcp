# ASA + Prodoscore — Q1 2026 Staffing Productivity Report

## Publisher Background

The American Staffing Association (ASA) partnered with Prodoscore — an ASA corporate partner — to produce this quarterly staffing productivity benchmark series. Prodoscore's platform passively captures activity data (calls, emails, CRM interactions, messaging) from recruiters across its staffing firm customer base, generating approximately 1.6 million data points per month. This is the Q1 2026 edition; prior editions cover Q4 2025 (published February 17, 2026) and earlier quarters.

---

## Recruiter Activity & Interaction Volume

- Recruiter call time reached **286 minutes per week** in Q1 2026 — the highest level on record
- Recruiter call time has **doubled since Q1 2024**
- Total recruiter interactions with candidates and clients **jumped 60% year-over-year** (Q1 2025 → Q1 2026)
- Increased AI tool usage is credited with freeing recruiter bandwidth for direct relationship-building activity

---

## AI Tool Adoption

- Average recruiter used **1.36 AI tools** in Q1 2026, up from **1.0 AI tools** in Q1 2024
- AI adoption is increasing across staffing firms, with more tools layered into recruiter workflows each quarter
- The data suggests AI augments rather than reduces direct recruiter interaction: higher AI usage correlates with higher call volume, not lower

---

## Top Performer vs. Average Recruiter Benchmarks

- Prodoscore's methodology explicitly benchmarks **average recruiter performance against a top-performer cohort**, providing actionable gap data
- Top performers consistently show higher activity levels across calls, emails, and CRM touches per week
- The report is designed to give staffing firms benchmarks to "gauge their own recruiters' productivity, measure performance, and identify growth opportunities"

---

## Industry Context

- 56% of staffing firms reported revenue growth in 2025 (compared to 40% the prior year); 13% saw revenue increase >25% YoY (from Bullhorn GRID 2026, published separately)
- The productivity surge comes amid a labor market where tight talent pools and flat job volumes remain the top dual challenge for staffing firms (third consecutive year)
- ASA CEO Stephen Dwyer: "Relationship-building continues to serve as a cornerstone of modern recruiting. As staffing firms continue to embrace AI, recruiters will have more time to focus on developing the connections that drive long-term growth."

---

## Key Conclusions

- **AI adoption doubles as a productivity amplifier, not a headcount reducer** — recruiters using more AI tools are spending *more* time talking to people, not less; the 60% jump in interactions is driven by AI freeing time from administrative tasks
- **Recruiter call time is at an all-time high** (286 min/week), signaling that the human relationship layer in staffing is intensifying even as automation scales
- **AI tool usage has grown 36% in two years** (1.0 to 1.36 tools per recruiter, Q1 2024 to Q1 2026), reflecting steady but measured adoption in the staffing sector
- **The top-performer gap is measurable and actionable** — Prodoscore's platform quantifies exactly how high-performing recruiters differ in activity patterns, giving firms a concrete benchmark to coach against
- **The quarterly cadence of this report makes it a reliable trend tracker** — compare future quarters to Q1 2026 baselines (286 min/week calls; 1.36 AI tools) to monitor whether the relationship-activity surge is sustained as AI matures
